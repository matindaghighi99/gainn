About us

Where neuroscience, engineering and the clinic share a bench.

Neuromodulation moves faster than any single discipline can follow. GAINN trains graduate students across both sides of it — the devices and the patients they treat — inside the CRANIA NeuroModulation Institute and its partner hospitals.

Cross-disciplinary coursework

Engineering, neuroscience and medicine, taught together.

Clinical immersion

Time inside the hospitals where stimulation is delivered.

Translation and industry

Skills to move neurotech from the lab toward the clinic.

The modalities trainees work across

Deep brain stimulation

Implanted electrodes reaching subcortical targets.

Spinal and peripheral

Interfaces for movement and chronic pain.

Non-invasive stimulation

Delivered through the skull, no implant.

Optogenetics

Light switching single populations of neurons.

Brain–computer interfaces

Neural activity decoded into control.

Machine learning

Models that turn recordings into decisions.
