01 — About us

One program where neuroscience, engineering and the clinic share a bench.

GAINN gives graduate trainees integrated training that bridges the clinical and engineering dimensions of neuromodulation, alongside exposure to the technologies shaping brain health research.

Cross-disciplinary coursework

Courses and workshops drawn from engineering, neuroscience and medicine.

Clinical immersion

Placements inside hospital environments where therapies are delivered.

Translation and industry

Professional skills and partnership training aimed at clinical use.
